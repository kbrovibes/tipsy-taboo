What it is

A deck of Taboo cards, minus the box

One phone goes round the table. Two to four teams. Each turn, one person has sixty seconds to get their team to say the word on the card — without saying any of the five words printed under it.

That is the whole game, and the five banned words are the whole difficulty. They are not five words that happen to be related; they are the five a person actually reaches for first. Which leaves you describing an elephant without trunk, grey, tusk, Africa or big.

How a turn works

The phone is the deck, the clock, the scoreboard and the buzzer. Nobody needs a pencil.

  1. Pass the phone to the clue-giver. Between turns the screen only says whose go it is, so the next card never leaks across the table.
  2. They tap start. Three, two, one, and the first card appears with the clock running.
  3. Swipe right when the team shouts it, left to pass. The other team listens for one of the five — if they hear one, the card is passed like any other.
  4. Check the recap. Every card of the turn is listed with what it scored, a −1 badge on the passes that cost. Tap any one to flip it — a panicked mis-swipe costs nothing if you catch it here.
  5. Bank the points and hand it on. Every team plays once per round. A tie at the end adds one more round.

House rules

Set before the first turn, changeable between games.

ThingHow it works
TeamsTwo to four, each playing exactly once per round.
Turn length60, 90 or 120 seconds.
Rounds3, 5, 8, or endless — endless runs until somebody calls it.
ScoringGot it +1 · free pass 0 · pass past your allowance −1.
Free passesNone, three a turn, or unlimited. Three is the default. After that, every pass is −1.
Taboo wordsAll five, in any form — if “Swim” is banned, so is “swimming”. Called out, the card is simply passed.
TiesA tie after the last round adds one more round. Repeat as needed.
AppearanceLight, dark or auto. Auto is the default and follows your phone.

The referee link

Optional, and the single best reason to have a second phone at the table.

Open the menu during a game and you get a four-letter code and a QR. On another phone, open /watch, type the four letters or scan the code, and that phone becomes the other team’s referee: the live card, the clock, the scores, and one enormous TABOO! button. Pressing it passes the current card on the game phone — the same single action, just from the other end of the table.

It is a broadcast channel and nothing more. No game is created, no code is registered, nothing is written to a database at either end — when the game ends, the code stops meaning anything. If the feature is not configured on the deployment, the app simply does not offer it and the game plays exactly as before.

The cards

6,280 cards across four difficulty levels and eight themed packs, written in parallel by Claude sub-agents against a spec in data/SPEC.md, then put through a validator in scripts/build-decks.mjs that rejects anything breaking it.

  • 🍹 Medium — 1,041
  • 🌶️ Hard — 1,021, the most-played level
  • 💀 Insane — 1,072

  • 🏏 Cricket
  • 📺 90s India
  • 🍿 Movies (world)
  • 🎲 The Big Mix

The eight themed packs hold 2,044 cards between them, 240 to 346 each, and all play at medium difficulty.

A ban may never contain the target, any word of it, or a four-letter stem of it, so “Swimming” can never ban “Swimmer”. No target repeats inside a deck, and a word that landed in two decks stays in the easier one. Every deck is family-friendly; Kids is stricter again.

Each deck is dealt like a real one — shuffled, then drawn front to back. Your phone remembers which cards it has already played, per deck and across games, so a second round tonight cannot hand you the same word twice.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone. Team names, scores, house rules, your appearance choice and the record of which cards this phone has played all live in your browser’s own localStorage. They are never uploaded, never backed up and never seen by anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes the lot.

The referee link is the one exception, and only while you are using it: the game phone broadcasts the current card and scores over a Supabase Realtime channel, and the referee phone sends a TABOO! back. Nothing on that channel is stored at either end.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. There is no sign-up, no tracking script, no cookie banner, because there are no cookies to warn you about. The whole game is a static site that works with the aeroplane mode on.
